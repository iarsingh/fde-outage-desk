# Discovery — Cedar Grid

The user is the storm-desk lead. During the last storm they worked a 400-customer feeder for half an hour while a smaller feeder with a registered life-safety account sat in the list.

Constraint from their communications team: this screen does not text or call customers. Constraint from dispatch: it does not assign a crew. It only ranks and suggests a crew count.

Out of scope: switching devices in the field, estimated restoration time promises, and any customer address on the card. The sample has no street addresses for that reason.
